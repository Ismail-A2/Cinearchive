How do I get a TMDB key?
  1. Go to themoviedb.org/signup, create a free account, and confirm your email address.
  2. Sign in, then open themoviedb.org/settings/api. You can also reach it from your profile picture, then Settings, then API.
  3. Choose Create to request an API key, pick Developer, and accept the terms.
  4. Fill in the form. Use “Your CineArchive” as the application name, the web address of this app as the URL, and a short summary such as “Personal film collection tracker”. Choose personal, non-commercial use. TMDB’s form may look slightly different.
  5. Copy the API Key, the shorter one of about 32 characters. The long “API Read Access Token” also works.
  6. Come back here, paste it into the box above, and tap Save key.
  7. Tap Match posters for my films to add artwork to anything you have already added.
